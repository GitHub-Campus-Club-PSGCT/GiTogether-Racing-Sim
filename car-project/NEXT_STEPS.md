# F1 RUSH — NEXT STEPS: STAGE 4 & BEYOND

---

## Stage 4: Visual Polish & Atmosphere

### 1. Objective
Enhance the visual fidelity, environmental depth, and atmosphere of the circuit to match the high-detail quality of the Mercedes F1 W17 car and trackside venue.

### 2. Planned Features for Stage 4:
1. **Dynamic Exhaust & Brake Smoke Particles**:
   - Subtle tire smoke puffs when drifting or hard braking from high speed.
2. **Track Surface Detailing**:
   - Racing line rubber tire marks on the asphalt through corner apexes.
   - 150m, 100m, 50m distance braking boards placed along the runoff perimeter approaching Turn 1 and Turn 6 Hairpin.
3. **Lighting & Shadows Fine-Tuning**:
   - Soft ambient occlusion / hemisphere lighting balance for deeper car reflections.
   - PBR roughness and metalness tuning on circuit kerbs and barriers.
4. **Cinematic Depth of Field & Horizon**:
   - Atmospheric depth haze gradient matching F1 Grand Prix broadcast aesthetics.
